# file-hands
move stuff with just your hands. pinch to grab, move to drag, release to drop. no keyboard, no mouse — just your hand in front of the camera.

uses mediapipe for hand tracking + a tight pinch detector normalized by hand size. gestures stay stable, smooth, & predictable.

soon it’ll support quick actions, window moves, simple commands, & other small ops you’d normally click for.

this is the start of a gesture layer to desktop computing that'll be faster, more natural & more fun to use.
